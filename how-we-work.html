How we work

A step-by-step, measured process.

Every farm is different, so we never start with a large deployment: we start by understanding your land, then we prove the result on a pilot, and only then do we scale up.

  1. 1
    1 to 2 weeks

    First contact

    You tell us about your farm: location, crops, area, soil, water sources and irrigation system, and what you want to achieve. One of our business developers assesses with you whether our solution fits, and proposes the next step.

  2. 2
    2 to 6 weeks

    Agronomic study: soil and climate

    Our agronomists study your soil (texture, depth, infiltration, salinity), your climate (rainfall, temperatures, evaporation), your crops and your irrigation. Where needed, they visit the farm and take soil samples. You receive a study report with a tailored application protocol and the expected benefits for your farm.

  3. 3
    One season, sometimes two

    Pilot

    We apply the hydro-retainer on a representative part of your farm, with our machinery and protocol, next to an untreated control area managed in the same way. This is the only fair way to measure what the solution does on your land.

  4. 4
    Throughout the season

    Monitoring and measurement

    Throughout the season, soil-moisture sensors and field visits track water use and crop development on treated and control plots. At harvest, we measure yield and quality. You receive a results report and the data behind it.

  5. 5
    Multi-year

    Large-scale deployment

    Based on the measured results, we plan the roll-out across your farm: product supply, application with our machines or trained teams, ongoing agronomic follow-up and renewal planning. Our teams stay with you season after season.

Every farm is different. That is why every project starts with a study, and every deployment starts with a pilot.
