The challenge

Water is now the limit

Agriculture uses around 70% of the world's freshwater. Rainfall is becoming more erratic, aquifers are falling and droughts last longer.

For a growing number of farms, it is no longer land that limits what they can grow. It is water. A large share of every irrigation never reaches the plant: it drains below the roots or evaporates in the heat. RainCatcher stops that loss where it happens, in the soil.

Our approach

Three components, one result

A hydro-retainer alone delivers only part of its potential. Machinery alone does nothing to the soil. Advice alone changes nothing in the field. RainCatcher is the only company that brings all three together.

Hydro-retainer

A patented, potassium-based hydro-retainer that absorbs up to 230 times its own weight in water and releases it to the roots.

+

Agronomy

Dose, depth and timing set for each crop, soil and climate by our field agronomists.

+

Machinery

Dedicated machines, developed with French equipment manufacturers, place the product precisely and quickly.

The right product, at the right dose, at the right depth, at the right time, for each crop, soil and climate.
