2.1 · Our purpose

Our purpose

Optimising agricultural production through the optimal use of water is our reason for being.

Water stress is one of the defining challenges of this century. Across the Mediterranean, the Middle East and North Africa, droughts are no longer exceptional events: they have become part of the farming calendar. Groundwater is being pumped faster than it recharges, irrigation quotas are being cut, and farmers are asked to feed more people with less water every year.

RainCatcher was created to answer that challenge at the most practical level possible: the root zone of each plant. We give farmers a way to hold water where it is useful, so that every cubic metre of irrigation or rainfall produces more crop, more reliably. We work mainly with large farms, agro-industrial groups and public agricultural institutions, because that is where each percentage point of water saved has the biggest effect on food security, on farm income and on the resources of a whole region.

2.2 · Our story

From a desert trial to a deployed solution

Born in the desert

RainCatcher started in Dubai, in one of the driest farming environments on earth. Date palm growers there face the same question every summer: how to keep trees productive when every drop of water is pumped from deep wells or produced by desalination at great cost. Our first answer was a patented potassium-based hydro-retainer, placed in the root zone of the palms, which captured irrigation water and released it slowly to the roots.

From a product to a method

The first trials taught us an essential lesson: the product is only as good as the way it is applied. On the surface, it is exposed to sun and wind; at the wrong depth or dose, it misses the roots. We therefore built two further capabilities around the hydro-retainer. The first is an agronomic method that sets dose, depth and timing for each crop, soil and climate. The second is a range of dedicated machines, developed with French equipment manufacturers, that place the product precisely and quickly, whether under a row of wheat or around the roots of a mature tree.

Proven in the field

Since 2019, RainCatcher has tested its solution on cereals, cotton, date palms, olives, vegetables and other crops, in climates ranging from hyper-arid desert to Mediterranean conditions. The solution was validated by the International Center for Biosaline Agriculture (ICBA), awarded the Solar Impulse Foundation “Efficient Solution” label, and in 2025 delivered one of its most striking results: a cotton crop in Hatay, one of Türkiye's driest regions, whose yield almost doubled.*

Building the company

In 2026 RainCatcher entered a new phase. Its shareholders strengthened the company's capital and governance to move from trials to commercial deployment. The group is organised around its head office in Dubai and its subsidiary RainCatcher Türkiye, and it is developing projects with farms and institutions in Morocco and across the Middle East and North Africa.

*Results observed in specific trials. Every farm and every crop is assessed through a preliminary agronomic study and will achieve its own results. See our full disclaimer.
