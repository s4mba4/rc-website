Our results

Seven years of trials, measured against a control.

Since 2019, RainCatcher has tested its solution on many crops and in many climates, from the hyper-arid deserts of the Gulf to the Mediterranean plains of Türkiye and North Africa. Every trial follows a documented protocol comparing treated and untreated plots, so that the results reflect what the solution actually does in the field.

5.2 · Case study

Cotton in Hatay, Türkiye (2025)

The context

Hatay, in southern Türkiye, is one of the driest farming regions of the country. The farm combined clay soils, extreme summer heat and irrigation infrastructure that had been damaged, making each irrigation both scarce and costly.

What we did

After a study of soil and climate, our team applied the hydro-retainer under the cotton rows with the RCSeeder®, alongside an untreated control area managed in the same way.

The results

The treated cotton yielded 4.88 tonnes per hectare, against 2.53 tonnes in the control area, an increase of 93%. The net gain for the grower was about 1,180 US dollars per hectare, and the investment was recovered within the first season.*

5.3 · Case study

Date palms in Dubai, United Arab Emirates

The context

In the hyper-arid conditions of the Gulf, date palms are irrigated from deep wells or desalinated water, at high cost in water and energy. Summer temperatures above 45 °C push water needs to their maximum.

What we did

The hydro-retainer was injected around the root systems of the palms, at the depth where the active roots draw water.

The results

Water savings of up to 87% were reported, with yield gains of at least 30%.*

5.5 · Independent validation

Validated by ICBA

The RainCatcher solution has been validated by the International Center for Biosaline Agriculture (ICBA), the Dubai-based international research centre dedicated to agriculture in water-scarce and saline environments. This independent validation confirms the agronomic value of our approach in some of the harshest growing conditions on earth.
